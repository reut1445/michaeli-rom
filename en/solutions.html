Our Solutions

Whether you have decided to divorce, your relationship is in crisis, or you have reached a crossroads and are unsure how to proceed, we will help you navigate the process safely and correctly.

Our mediation practice offers several options tailored to your unique circumstances. Each can help you reach agreement even on the most sensitive and complex issues and begin a new chapter in family life that suits you and your children.

01

Divorce Mediation

Divorce mediation takes place through a series of meetings that encourage open, mutual, respectful dialogue. The aim is to reach agreements on issues such as child custody, education and parental responsibility, housing, property, child support, and more.

Every solution is subject to mutual consent. The process also helps rebuild a sense of security, personal well-being, and certainty about the future, reducing the need for prolonged, costly power struggles that cause emotional exhaustion and distress for children.

Mediation results in a divorce agreement that, once legally approved as required, enables you to begin a new chapter as co-parents.

02

Marital Reconciliation

A marital reconciliation agreement gives a couple the opportunity to restore their relationship and recover from a crisis by defining the terms of their shared life and relationship going forward. The process takes place in a safe, supportive, empathetic setting, emphasizing rebuilding trust, finding creative solutions to conflicts and differences that have developed over time, and establishing mutual rules and commitments that significantly reduce them.

The process allows couples to assess their future freely and with greater confidence, and to formalize the decisions reached in mediation in an agreement.

Schedule a free consultation and guidance call
03

Marital Reconciliation Agreement with Divorce as an Alternative

This is an especially sensitive process designed for couples at a crossroad: on the one hand, a genuine opportunity to rebuild the relationship; on the other, a clear safety net if reconciliation does not succeed.

Through a series of open, transparent discussions, we develop a dual framework defining both the terms of reconciliation and the terms of divorce. With an informed choice and greater control over the outcome, couples can focus on repairing their relationship without fear of the consequences of a final separation or future power struggles.
