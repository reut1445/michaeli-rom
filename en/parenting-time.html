Options for Parenting Time Arrangements

In this article

One of the issues you will agree upon is child custody and parenting time. This important decision will affect your everyday lives, your children’s lives, and your relationships with your children and one another. At first glance, the subject may be confusing because there seem to be many options. In practice, answering a few questions makes the decision easier. Understanding the alternatives can help you reach the arrangement best suited to you, with less time and stress.

What Should You Consider?

  • Number of days — Do you want equal shared custody, shared custody without alternating Thursdays, or for one parent to have more days?
  • Number of transitions — Fewer moves between homes make transportation easier and reduce disruption for children, but mean longer consecutive stays with one parent.
  • Overnight stays — You can decide that on certain days (such as Saturdays) children will visit a parent without staying overnight.
  • Your individual needs — For example, the days on which you work from home.

In Summary

First consider how many days you want the children to spend with each parent, and select the appropriate schedule. Then decide whether shorter consecutive stays or fewer transitions are more important and choose accordingly. Less common alternatives, such as alternating full weeks, are also possible. Parents sometimes agree that children may go to one parent’s home after school even on the other parent’s scheduled day.
