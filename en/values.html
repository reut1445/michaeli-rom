Our Core Values

01

Children Come First

Our goal is to ensure that your children are not caught in conflict between their parents, now or in the future, and that they grow up in a stable, healthy, supportive environment at every stage.

02

Agreements That Stand the Test of Time

Drawing on years of experience, we examine every solution from a broad, long-term personal, family, and financial perspective to craft practical and lasting agreements.

03

Support That Reduces Emotional Strain

We conduct the process sensitively and attentively, drawing on extensive professional and personal experience to ease the burden of this challenging period enabling calm and balanced decision-making. We see the creation of a “new partnership” between the parties as our mission.
