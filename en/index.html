The Mediation Process

On average, about five weekly meetings are required. The duration depends on the agreement’s complexity, the number and scope of unresolved issues, and the parties’ willingness to cooperate.

Introductory Meeting

An in-depth explanation of the process and available options, with answers to initial questions.

Starting with Listening

Identifying individual and shared needs and goals (emotional, family-related, parental, and financial).

Identifying Needs and Resources

Reviewing the issues to be addressed in the agreement and finding solutions, while understanding each party’s future needs, identifying shared assets and resources, and ensuring their fair division.

Building the Way Forward

Developing practical solutions and agreements tailored to your needs, goals, and new circumstances.

Drafting the Agreement

Preparing a complete, precise, comprehensive agreement reflecting the understandings reached during the mediation.

Separation is far more than a legal agreement

It involves emotional, family, parenting, and financial layers, often viewed very differently by each partner.

Particularly at such a sensitive and complex stage, having both a female and a male mediator in the room provides significant value.

Driven by this approach, we, Keren Michaeli and Amnon Rom, founded our mediation practice.

Our combination brings two complementary perspectives, attentive to the needs and sensitivities of each party. It creates a more supportive, secure, and balanced space in which everyone feels heard.

We bring many years of experience in law and management, together with personal and parenting experience. We conduct every mediation fairly, working toward a solid foundation for a new partnership after separation.
